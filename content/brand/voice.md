# Albuc voice

Albuc is a personal library and notes product. Organic GTM teaches from a real book, then invites people to keep what they read.

- Warm paper, serif, unhurried.
- One idea per beat. No listicles of empty verbs.
- Short quotes only. Always: book title + author.
- CTA is the product name **Albuc** and albuc.com. Never invent another name.
- Shorts: spoken VO + picture B-roll + captions. Not text cards. Not a talking head.
- Long YouTube: spoken VO + B-roll. Same rule.
