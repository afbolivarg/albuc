# Shortform VO script (9:16)

Duration: ~33s. Spoken voiceover + B-roll + captions.
Not a talking-head. Not text cards only.

Source: The Commonplace Book — A. Reader

## Read this

1. (0.0s) You don't have a reading problem. You have a keeping problem.
2. (3.2s) You highlighted half the book. You cannot teach one page of it.
3. (7.6s) The ideas leaked because nothing in the week asked you to use them
4. (12.6s) If you cannot say it, you did not keep it
5. (17.8s) Remember this: the book is not finished when you close it
6. (23.2s) Ask the shelf later. That is the whole practice.
7. (28.2s) Don't just read. Build ideas. Keep the book in Albuc.

## Delivery
Warm, unhurried, close-mic. One breath per line. No background music under the last line.
